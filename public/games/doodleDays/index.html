Doodle Days

The diary and games of Tilly Marsh, age 10.

Doodle Dash

Help Tilly get through four worlds and sixteen levels: the hallway, the library, a field trip and a very icy snow day. Crumpled homework, runaway erasers, pencil hoppers and paper airplanes are in her way.

Doodle Dash field notes

Sticky-note boxes

Bonk them from below. Most hold a star. Some hold a power-up.

Pudding Cup

Makes Tilly bigger. Big Tilly can smash textbook bricks and survive one extra hit.

Spitball Straw

Hold it and press Run to fire bouncing spitballs. Works on everything, including pencils.

Pencil Hoppers

Sharp end up. Don't stomp them. Jump over or spit at them.

Stomp combos

Chain stomps without landing to double your points each time. Hold Jump on a stomp to bounce higher.

Springs

Land on a spring to launch over tall lockers. Holding Jump isn't needed.

Moving rulers

Ride them across wide gaps. Wait for one to come back if you miss it.

Snow Day

World 4 is icy. You slide, so start stopping early.

Random level

Press R on the map (or tap the blue button) for a brand-new level every time.

Stars

Collect 50 for an extra life. Your best star count per level is saved in this browser.
