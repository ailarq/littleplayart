🌸 How to play

← → or A / D to run. Space, ↑ or W to jump (hold for higher). Esc or P pauses. On touch screens use the on-screen buttons.

Jump on critters to bop them. Bump ? and brick blocks from below. Some blocks are hidden! Collect coins and all 3 ⭐ stars, then reach the pink flag.

🫧 Bubble = blocks one hit · ⭐ Star = invincible · 🦋 Wings = hold jump to glide · 🍑 Peach = speed boost
